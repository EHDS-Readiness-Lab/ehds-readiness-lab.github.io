# Changes

## 10 November 2026, revision 0.2.1

- Removed the four-tile symbol from the home page header. The site now uses a text-only masthead with the existing restrained colour palette.
- Replaced the similar four-tile browser favicon with a minimal typographic DQ favicon.
- No changes to the source reports, interactive resources, proposed requirements or navigation.

## 10 November 2026, revision 0.2

- Confirmed and incorporated Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2098 across the home page, interactive timeline, comparative analysis and draft specification.
- Distinguished the adopted dataset-description metadata requirements from forthcoming Article 78 quality-label technical specifications.
- Replaced the readiness status **Ready** with **Evidence available** in explanatory text, derived-state table, demonstrator, RDF and the versioned vocabulary. No quality threshold is implied.
- Clarified that release-level evidence is a proposed design choice; the legal label is dataset-related.
- Separated draft exports from draft validation; incomplete CSV and Turtle drafts are exportable and are marked `draft-unattested`.
- Added checks and fields for E3 method and input locations, variable scope for `LR-04` / `LR-09`, and named review of non-applicability.
- Added a publishable RDF vocabulary in `vocabulary/label-readiness-v0.2.ttl` and notes about namespace stability.
- Removed the unavailable companion catalogue proposal as a normative dependency while retaining an explanatory placeholder.
- Corrected language about the HIQA pilot participant counts to avoid implying seven distinct organisations.

## Limits

No formal accessibility certification, authenticated attestation, production-grade verification of evidence or live regulatory conformance testing is claimed.
